# Goal Replay

A static web app that replays every 2022 World Cup goal in 3D from real tracking data. Pick a player, pick a goal, and watch a ~21.5-second clip (15 s before the shot, 6.5 s after) with pause, scrub, 0.25x/0.5x/1x speed, and an orbit camera with presets. Full brief: [docs/brief.md](docs/brief.md).

Next: go through the accuracy report's 53 flagged clips (mostly PFF player tracks that StatsBomb and the tracked ball contradict), fix what Zayd reports, merge, then Weekend 5 (mobile check, Vercel deploy, README, demo GIF).

## How Zayd works

- He watches clips in `cd web && npm run dev` and reports problems by player and minute. Short, direct answers, no buzzwords.
- Named clips are examples: fix the cause for every clip, no clip names or special cases in pipeline code. Prefer measured data (StatsBomb events and freeze frames, the tracked ball where the feed has it) over inferred data; never invent actions. Report counts across all clips and the most-changed clips per rule.
- After pipeline changes: `python pipeline/build_all.py` then `python -m pytest`. Problems should be 0; the review list is the accuracy report's flagged clips. If goals were added or removed: `build_all.py`, `shot_placement.py`, `build_all.py` (the placement script reads `clips/index.json` and the clips).

## Pipeline order (`build_clip` in `pipeline/cut_clip.py`)

`player_lists` (extra time: StatsBomb votes, `relabel.py`) → `statsbomb_shot` (the shot frame: ball nearest StatsBomb's shot location within ±1 s of PFF's or StatsBomb's shot; from here goal_index is the shot) → `drop_out_of_play` → `pick_ball_source` (the feed with its ball at StatsBomb's shot spot wins) → `borrow_gaps` → `estimate_gaps` → shooter onto StatsBomb's shot spot (≤5 m, else `try_swap`) → penalties: `pin_ball`, `place_players` → `place_keepers` → `correct_goal_mouth` → `smooth_jumps` → `find_contacts` → `align_contacts` → `clip_statsbomb` → `merge_touches` → `find_restarts`, `apply_restarts` (StatsBomb timing and free-kick spot), `place_keepers` again → shot contact → `ease_to_freeze_frame` → `rebuild_dribbles` → `anchor_frames` → `apply_physics` → `straighten_free_flight` → dead balls re-drawn → clearance check → `fly_shot` (to StatsBomb's crossing point; `into_net`) → `meet_touches` (≤5 m moves, `try_swap`) → `enforce_touch_rule` (no added touches when StatsBomb covers the clip) → `shot_pose` → `plan_dive` → `beat_keeper` → `redraw_roll_out` → `violations` → `clear_bodies` → `limit_player_speeds` → `clear_bodies` → `check_clip`.

The ball before the shot is only ever: a physics flight between two touches, a rebuilt dribble, a carry, or a dead ball at a restart. Any turn, sudden speed change, lift with no touch within 3 frames, or one-frame jump over 45 m/s (even at a touch) is a rule break (`touch_rule.py`).

## Accuracy report (`accuracy.py`, every clip, on the clip as written)

A clip is flagged, and goes on the review list, if: a touch is more than 1.2 m from the player; the ball turns with no touch; the keeper dives before the kick or away from where the ball reaches him; a StatsBomb on-ball action before the shot (not the goal itself) has no touch by that player within 0.5 s; the shot crosses more than 0.6 m from StatsBomb's point; a player moves faster than 10.5 m/s over 0.2 s; a keeper is outside his posts in his own box with the ball in it before the kick and either more than 2 m from and wider than StatsBomb's freeze-frame spot (last second before the kick) or more than 2 m outside the post line away from the ball; the ball at the kick is more than 2 m from StatsBomb's shot location ("shot spot"); or the ball passes through a player's body (legs 0.18 m, torso 0.22 m, head 0.12 m radius) with no touch ("body pass"). build_all prints clips/findings per check and every flagged clip; the review list is these flags (53 clips now).

## Sep 27 changes

- **Ball physics** (`ball_physics.py`, `ball_flight.py`): between touches the ball is a simulated kick (gravity, drag, bounces, rolling), solved to arrive at the next touch at the right time and height.
- **Touches lined up with the tracking** (`align_contacts` in `contacts.py`): PFF event times are often off by ~0.5 s. A touch is kept if the ball is within 1.5 m of the player, else moved within ±0.4 s to where the tracked ball is at his feet, else (if the feed had a gap there) the ball is placed at his feet, else dropped. Shot touches are always kept; touches after the shot are dropped.
- **Dribbles** (`dribble.py`): PFF logs a dribble as one carry. Stretches where one player keeps a low ball get a touch every stride (contacts with `"s": 1`), pushes rolled with physics, the carrier's track smoothed. If the rebuild isn't believable, the stretch is written as `carries` and the viewer holds the ball at his feet.
- **Viewer**: throw-in pose (`animateThrow`) and scissor/overhead kick (`animateVolley`) in `rig.ts`, driven from `restarts` and `"v": 1` contacts in `Replay.tsx`. `web/src/ballTrack.ts` smooths the ball (fills held coordinates, quadratic fit between touches, cubic interpolation with sharp corners at touches/bounces). Touch pull ±0.12 s, never more than 1.5 m. The ball rolls with its speed on the ground and only spins gently in the air. Cameras (`playCam.ts`): Broadcast = fixed TV wide shot that pans and zooms (default); Follow play = medium height behind the play with a shot cam behind the shooter. Carry fallback in `Replay.tsx`.
- **Penalties** (`penalty.py`): PFF marks them with `gameEvents.setpieceType == "P"` on the shot (16 clips). Until the kick (`goalFrame`, which matches the ball leaving the spot to ~0.1 s) the ball sits on the spot at z 0; everyone but the taker and keeper is moved to the nearest point outside the area and 9.15 m from the spot (+0.25 m); the keeper stands on his line between the posts; the taker is eased onto the ball (0.5 m) over a 2 s run-up. The legal point follows each player frame to frame (a player PFF puts on the spot would otherwise flip around the arc). After the kick everyone blends back over max(0.5 s, move / 5 m/s). No touches, dribbles or carries before the kick. The keeper is whichever GK is within 6 m of the goal line.
- **Shot placement** (`shot_aim` in `cut_clip.py`, `aim_point` in `goal_mouth.py`): PFF events have no end location in the goal mouth, only the height third (`shotInitialHeightType`). `pipeline/shot_placement.py` matches every clip to StatsBomb Open Data and writes `shot_placement.json` (clip -> crossing y, z on our pitch, shot technique, freeze-frame keeper; 163 of 166 placed, the 3 own goals have none). Precedence: a hand-set `"aim"` in `overrides.json` (none now), then StatsBomb, then PFF's height third. The crossing (and a goal-line clearance's closest point) is moved there ("aimed"). `smooth_jumps` only touches frames up to the shot (and, after a goal-line clearance, the tracked path from the end of the clearance blend), so it can't glide over the aimed crossing; running it before the correction glided over real crossings instead (5 more synthesized shots). A jump just before the feed loses the ball for good is dropped (Weah). `fly_shot` also stretches shots faster than 35 m/s (de Jong v Qatar: 23 m in 7 frames). Final crossings are within 0.3 m of StatsBomb for 152 of 163 clips (worst 0.52 m). The review list's 3 m check uses `track_shift` (wide/high/clearance only). StatsBomb is credited in README.md (their licence requires it).
- **Extra-time label swap** (`player_lists` in `cut_clip.py`): in periods 3-4 of the final (10517) and Japan v Croatia (10506) each list's shirt numbers are its own team's players on the pitch (they follow that team's subs), but the positions are the other team's. Undone by swapping the positions and pairing labels: same number where both teams have it, the rest by position group (`label_pairing`). Checked on logged touches: the nearest player to the ball is a teammate 86% / 69% of the time (79-83% in normal time; 14% / 31% unswapped), keepers defend the right goals. Individual names are only partly recoverable: touchers within 3 m of the ball 30% (58% in normal time), 42% for same-number pairs, 19% for position pairs, so some outfield names in extra time are wrong. Messi 108' and Mbappé 118' are right (both #10, keepers).
- Ferran Torres 53:41 v Costa Rica is excluded (neither feed has the shot). Messi 107:57 v France: fixed by the extra-time label swap (Messi 4.5 m from the ball at the shot, was 33 m).

## Sep 27 evening: rules that run on every clip

No per-clip overrides or clip names in pipeline code; the named clips are only test cases.

- **Extra-time names** (`relabel.py`, `swapped_votes` in `cut_clip.py`): slots in the swapped periods switch players every few minutes, so names come from inside each clip's window only. Every StatsBomb event there votes for the nearest tracked player of its team (within 5 m); a slot with ≥2 votes and ≥60% of them takes that name, the rest keep `label_pairing`. StatsBomb time → video time: per-period median offset between the same player's touches in both feeds. Calibrated on Morocco v Spain normal time: a clear vote is right 95% of the time; toucher-within-3 m ceiling there 75%. Extra-time windows (both matches): 29% → 47%. Needs `data/statsbomb/lineups/{3869685,3869219}.json` (fetch with curl).
- **Touch rule** (`touch_rule.py`, `ball_rules.py`): before the goal-line crossing the ball only turns (>15°), changes speed suddenly or rises at a touch within reach (1.5 m; bounces allowed). Fixes, in order: touches before the shot get the ball within reach; a break with a player of either team within 2.5 m becomes his touch (`"s": 2`; foot, head over 1.3 m, hands for a keeper in his area); otherwise the stretch is flown as one kick from the previous touch to the next. Kicks are solved (`solve_to_height`) to arrive at a height the touching part can reach; a straight line only if no kick fits. Checked and fixed on the rounded values that get written. `simulate` interpolates between solver steps.
- **The shot** (`find_kick`, `fly_shot`): the kick is the last frame (up to 1 s before PFF's shot) with the tracked ball within 1.5 m of the shooter. The ball is at his foot (head, hands) and flies as one kick straight to the aimed crossing (StatsBomb), 20-35 m/s (headers 11+, hands 8+), then into the net. A shooter tracked more than 3 m from the ball is eased onto it (no faster than 4 m/s on top of his run) instead of moving the ball (46 clips; Blind 45:36 is 17 m off in the tracking). Touches after the kick are dropped unless a logged opponent has the ball within reach (deflection; none now). Replaces `retime_shot`/`physics_shot`.
- **Restarts** (`restarts.py`): `setpieceType` T throw-in, C corner, G goal kick, F free kick, K kick-off (D drop ball ignored, P is `penalty.py`); `OUT` events with `outType` T (touchline) / W (goal line), or the last event before a free kick (the foul). A ball that goes out in the clip rolls on, stops out of play, and is hidden if the restart is elsewhere; if it went out before the clip, the ball is dead from frame 0. Throw-in: at the thrower's feet behind the line, lifted, held over his head 1 s, thrown (hands touch). Others: still on the arc / in the goal area / at the free-kick spot / centre spot, taker behind it. A restart is taken at the last frame (−2 s to +0.5 s from the event) the tracked ball is at the spot. Dead stretches are held like carries. Clips carry `restarts` (the viewer's throw pose uses `hold`).
- **Keepers** (`keepers.py`): PFF's track is kept unless implausible, then corrected only as far as needed (changing ≤7.5 m/s): out of his area with the ball not near (a ≥1 s run out with the ball within 8 m is sweeping, kept), outside the posts while the ball is in his box, or >8 m from the ball-to-goal-centre line. Left as tracked around his own touches. At the shot he is eased over 1 s onto StatsBomb's freeze-frame spot (`shot_placement.json` "keeper", 147 clips; PFF median 1.0 m off), held there 1.8 s through the dive, then eased back. `plan_dive` (clip `keeperDive`, `kickFrame`): toward the crossing, 0.2 s reaction, late and partial if the ball arrives sooner, stretch capped at 2.5 m (beyond: dives and misses), within 0.6 m a block. Viewer: dive height follows the ball; keepers face the ball unless sprinting, side-step in a ready stance, set-hop before the kick (`animateKeeper`, `animateBlock`). Penalty keepers: `penalty.py` until the kick.
- **How the goal is struck** (`volleys.py`, `shot_pose`): only from StatsBomb's shot technique (`shot_placement.json` "technique"), only on the goal's shot. Normal / Lob / Diving Header: nothing (no dive-header pose). Half Volley: standing half-volley pose. Volley: standing volley, or scissor if the arriving ball is 0.9-1.4 m and he's within 25° of side-on to the shot. Overhead Kick: bicycle (no 2022 goal). Scissors: Mbappé 80:58, Richarlison 72:54; bicycles 0. Contact `"v"`: scissor / bicycle / volley / half. The scissor uses the original `animateVolley`; bicycle is the same flat on his back.
- **Ball in the net** (`net.py`): past the line no tracking; `into_net` carries on with the flight's exact velocity at the line, back netting (2 m) gives and stops it, side/roof netting stop it, drops and rolls to rest. `across_the_line` reports the turn/speed change; only side-netting hits turn >5° in the first frame. A goal-line clearance needs a logged defender touch (not the shooter) within 1 s of the deepest point: only Messi 107:57.
- Restarts are never taken after the goal frame (a direct free kick is the shot).
- Current counts: rule breaks 0; 37 restarts; 213 of 318 keeper tracks corrected; 152 dives, 14 blocks; 2 scissor kicks; balls floating with no gravity 0 (throw-in holds aside).

## Accuracy run: StatsBomb touches, meeting the ball, keeper timing

- **StatsBomb touches** (`statsbomb.py`): `match_events` loads a match (per-period offsets from same-player touches); `clip_events` picks a clip's window and finds its own shift (±10 s of the offset, 0.1 s steps, then 0.01 s): the one where StatsBomb's on-ball locations best match the tracked players and the tracked ball (misses capped at 15 m, 1.5 m per second of shift as a prior). PFF's video clock drifts within a period (33 goals >1 s), and pinning StatsBomb's goal to the kick matched the tracking worse overall. Locations: 120x80 scaled to 105x68, each team attacking x = 120. `merge_touches`: on-ball types (pass, receipt, clearance, shot, block, keeper touches, interception, dribble, recovery, miscontrol; not the goal itself, nor the scorer's actions within 0.3 s of the kick) become touches (`"sb"`), timed to the tracked ball within ±0.4 s and between their neighbours; a PFF touch by the same player within 0.3 s is the same touch. If the tracked ball and player agree with each other but not StatsBomb's spot, the touch is there (`"tr"`); else the ball is put at StatsBomb's spot and re-drawn. Touches no kick could connect (>35 m/s) are slid or dropped. High passes fly ≥3 m. Needs `data/statsbomb/lineups/*.json` for all 64 matches (curl).
- **The kick** (`find_kick`): within ±1 s of PFF's shot event, the last frame with the tracked ball within 1.5 m of the shooter from which it leaves at ≥8 m/s toward goal (PFF logs shots late and early: Paik 75:48 +0.6 s).
- **Every touch meets the player** (`meet_touches`, `limit_player_speeds`): >1.2 m apart and the ball well supported (a StatsBomb touch, or near the goal line): the player is eased onto it (≥0.5 s, ≤3 m/s on top of his run); over 10 m only if the tracked ball backs StatsBomb, else flagged. Otherwise the touch rule moves the ball. A goal-line clearance gets the defender's touch where the ball turns (Koundé, Messi 107:57). Every player is held to 9.5 m/s with his touch frames fixed. `_connectable` drops the less trusted of two touches no kick could connect (added < dribble push < PFF < StatsBomb; never the shot or restarts).
- **Scissor** (`animateScissor`): sideways roll ~50° in the air, ~70° landing on the hip, other leg first, kicking leg across at hip-to-chest height. Bicycle is `animateBicycle`. Scissors now: Mbappé 80:58, Richarlison 72:54, Koulibaly 69:29.
- **Keeper reaction** (`plan_dive`): judged where the ball comes level with him (the crossing for a keeper on his line). Take-off ≥ kick + 0.25 s and ≥ arrival − 0.55 s; late means partial; within 0.9 m a block/crouch at the ball's height (`animateBlock`); always toward the ball. 129 dives, 37 blocks, 0 wrong way.

The ball feed with its ball there wins over the 8 m rule. The shooter is eased onto the spot (≤5 m). The shot flies to StatsBomb's crossing point. Direct and StatsBomb-timed free kicks use StatsBomb's spot. The clip's goalFrame is the shot; eventFrame is PFF's event.
- **Locations**: StatsBomb x/y are yards inside the box (spot 11 m, posts 3.66 m), blended to 105 x 68 toward halfway and the touchlines.
- **Identities** (`identity.py`): never move a player more than 5 m to reach a touch. Beyond that, a teammate within 3.5 m of the ball means PFF mixed them up: swap their tracks over the stretch bounded by where they come within 2 m (or the clip ends), only if StatsBomb's events fit better (49 swaps). Else: flagged if the tracked ball backs StatsBomb, ball to the player if only StatsBomb does.
- **No invented touches**: with StatsBomb covering a clip, the touch rule joins breaks as one kick instead of adding touches.
- **Bodies**: `clear_bodies` nudges anyone the ball would pass through (≤~0.4 m, 0.3 s ease); `beat_keeper` puts a close shot past a blocking keeper (through the legs if low and central, else he stands aside within 1 m of StatsBomb's spot so it clears him by 10 cm, judged at the continuous closest approach); dives end 10 cm short of the ball. `animateBlock` shifts weight, reaches, recoils.
- **Keepers** re-run after restarts; the post-dive hold is 1.8 s, then the sprint cap applies.

## Architecture

- **Pipeline (Python, offline):** find goal events → cut the tracking frames for the clip window (one pass per tracking file) → convert to a standard pitch (105 × 68 m, centered at 0,0) → fill small gaps → goal-mouth correction → write one compact JSON clip per goal plus one goal index JSON. `pipeline/build_all.py` runs everything; `pipeline/cut_clip.py` cuts a single goal.
- **Clip format:** metadata (teams, colors, players) plus frames of `{t, ball x/y/z, player x/y by id}`, rounded to 2 decimals.
- **Viewer (browser):** React + Vite + TypeScript, three.js via react-three-fiber + drei. Interpolates between the two nearest frames on every animation frame.
- No backend, database, or API keys. Hosted statically (Vercel or GitHub Pages).

## Data notes

Findings from game 10517 (the final):

- Tracking runs at 29.97 fps (about 255k frames per match).
- The ball has a height (z). Some values are slightly negative, so clamp z to 0 or above.
- Match events to tracking frames with `game_event_id` on the tracking frame, which equals `gameEventId` on the event. Don't match on timestamps.
- Goals are shots with `possessionEvents.shotOutcomeType == "G"`. Penalty-shootout kicks are also labeled period 4, but so are real extra-time goals, so don't filter by period. A shootout kick is any goal whose `eventTime` comes after the last `gameEventType == "END"` event.
- All 22 players are present in every frame, since PFF estimates off-camera players. Only the ball goes missing (about 7% of in-play frames).
- Coordinates are already in meters on a 105 × 68 pitch centered at (0, 0).
- `goalT` (the goal event's tracking frame) is the moment of the shot, not the ball crossing the line. For Di María the ball crosses about 1.3 s later, so clips run 6.5 s past `goalT` (`AFTER_S`).
- After every goal PFF writes an `OUT` event with `outType` `H` or `A` (a goal for the home or away team), followed by the conceding team's kickoff (`setpieceType` `K`). An `OUT` goal marker with no goal shot from the scoring team is treated as an own goal, credited to the conceding team's last player on the ball (`pipeline/goals.py`). Across all 64 matches this finds 3 own goals (Enzo Fernández, Aguerd, Neuer).
- Goal-mouth correction (`pipeline/goal_mouth.py`): PFF loses the ball after most shots, and the tracked path can then miss the goal or stop short of it. After `goalFrame` there are three cases. (1) Goal-line clearance: the ball gets within 1.5 m of the line between the posts, then moves back at least 1 m before it crosses. This is Messi 108', where Koundé cleared it from behind the line and raw `balls` peaks ~0.9 m short. The path is shifted locally in x so its closest point is 0.3 m over the line, blending back to the real path over 0.5 s each side (never before the shot). The clearance is kept and the ball is not put in the net. (2) Crosses the line wide of the posts (|y| > 3.66) or over the bar (z > 2.44): the path is shifted to cross 0.3 m inside the nearest post and under the bar. The shift ramps linearly from 0 at the shot, so nothing jumps. After any crossing the ball eases 2 m into the net and rests on the ground there. (3) Never crosses: if the ball vanishes within 5 m of the goal mouth, it is carried straight in at 15 m/s. Otherwise the path is left as is and the clip gets `"needsReview": true`, with no invented long flights. The old velocity extrapolation from the last ball in the clip is gone; it flew Messi 108' ~45 m into the net in the last 0.3 s. Clips carry `"ballCorrected"` and `"needsReview"`.
- `ballsSmoothed` has no ball during a penalty run-up (ball on the spot) and can badly lag the raw `balls` field. At Messi's 108' goal in the final it sits on Messi's own (wrongly estimated, ~30 m off) position at the shot, while raw `balls` has it on the goal line. Raw `balls` is far more complete in the clip windows.
- Ball source is picked per clip (`pipeline/cut_clip.py`, `choose_ball_source`). Raw `balls` is used by default. The fallback is `ballsSmoothed` when the gap-filled raw ball at `goalFrame` is missing or more than `RAW_BALL_MAX_M` (8 m) from every player on the scorer's team. For an own goal that is the conceding team, whose player is on the ball. In the final that sends only Di María to smoothed: his raw ball is 13.3 m from any Argentina player. The others are 2.0–7.7 m. The report prints this distance for every clip, sorted, to check the threshold once all matches are in. Both feeds get the same gap fill, z clamp and pitch conversion, and goal-mouth correction runs on the chosen path. Clips carry `"ballSource": "smoothed" | "raw"`. `balls` is a list that holds 0 or 1 ball, and both feeds share the same z.
- Overrides (`pipeline/overrides.json`): a map of clip file name to `{"ballSource": "smoothed" | "raw", "note": "..."}`, which wins over the automatic source choice, or to `{"exclude": true, "note": "..."}`, which leaves the goal out of the clips and the index. Use it for clips the 8 m rule gets wrong, and keep the threshold as it is. Every entry needs a note plus a valid `ballSource` or `"exclude": true`, or the pipeline stops. The report marks overridden clips (with the automatic choice), lists overrides and exclusions, and an entry that matches no goal in a loaded match shows up under problems. Excluded goals still count toward the running score in the index. Current entry: `10517_6738451.json` (Mbappé 80:58 volley) is set to smoothed. Its raw ball is 7.7 m from the nearest France player, just under the threshold, and about 8 m from Mbappé at the shot, so it flew off without him.
- Disallowed goals: a goal shot (`shotOutcomeType == "G"`) with no `OUT` goal marker is a goal ruled out by VAR or offside. There are 24 across the tournament. They are left out of the clips and index and listed in the report under "disallowed (excluded)". This brings the total to the official 172.
- A goal can have no shooter: Bruno Fernandes v Uruguay (`3843_6578657`) is a cross (`CR`) marked `G` with `shooterPlayerId` empty. The scorer then falls back to the player on the ball (`gameEvents.playerId` / `playerName`).
- Game 10510 (CRO v BRA) has no extra-time tracking: frames stop at the end of period 2. Neymar 105+1' and Petković 117' are listed as "no tracking" (no clip, still counted in the running score). `read_windows` collects the periods it saw, so the report can tell "tracking ends before the goal's period" apart from "game_event_id on no frame". No goal needs a clock-based fallback yet.
- PFF `week` is the round: 1–3 group matchdays, 4 Round of 16, 5 Quarter-final, 6 Semi-final, 7 Third place, 8 Final. This is confirmed by the match counts, 16/16/16/8/4/2/1/1.
- Review queue: each index entry has a `review` list of reasons, empty if the clip looks fine. A clip is flagged if the ball doesn't go in (`needsReview`), a goal-mouth correction shifted it more than 3 m (`BIG_SHIFT_M`), or the raw source was chosen with the ball 6–8 m from the scoring team (`REVIEW_RAW_M` to `RAW_BALL_MAX_M`). The viewer shows a dot on flagged goals and has a "Needs review" filter. `build_all.py` deletes any clip JSON in `clips/` that isn't in the index.
- `ballsSmoothed` is pinned exactly onto the player on the ball at every event frame. It was 0.0 m from the scorer at `goalFrame` in all six goals in the final, and it runs in straight lines between events. That is why ball-to-shooter distance can't be used to judge the smoothed feed. The player positions are PFF estimates too: at penalty kicks the taker is 2–5 m from a raw ball that sits on the spot.
